Daily Review
9-Point Check
Form M-100CB · LHDVIR
Linehaul only
Check every form, every day
One report per power unit, per day of operation
Standard M-100CB booklet only
Completed by hand — no electronic edits
File same day · retain 6 months
1
Real Form

Standard M-100CB booklet, completed by hand. No electronic edits, no photocopied substitutes.

2
City of Inspection

Full city, town or village name. Ditto marks are acceptable only where the location is unchanged from the entry directly above.

3
Defect Box — Yes or No

Ticked for every unit listed — tractor, every trailer, dolly. Blank, or both boxes ticked, is non-compliant.

4
Time

Exact time of inspection, clearly marked am/pm or written in military time.

5
Date

Full month, day and year. Not just the day number.

6
Inspector’s Printed Name

Printed in full and legible. A signature alone does not satisfy this point.

7
Inspector’s Signature

Present and matching the printed name above.

8
Driver’s Declaration

Signed whenever someone other than the driver performed the inspection. May be left blank when the driver inspected.

9
Co-Driver Signature

Required on team operations only. Not applicable to single-driver runs.

If a form fails any point — or doesn’t arrive at all
  1. Contact the TSP the same day. Do not hold errors for a weekly batch.
  2. Return the form for correction and track it until the corrected copy is physically in the file. Found is not fixed.
  3. Missing hand-in? Flag it to the LH Manager the same day — the TSP conversation is theirs to have.
  4. Persistent non-compliance escalates to a Business Discussion, recorded in TRACS.
  5. Defect box says “Yes”? Major → unit does not roll, contact FleetNet immediately. Minor → road-legal, flag and prioritise return to MNTR. Trailers and dollies only — tractor defects are the TSP’s to action.
    Telling them apart: Schedule 1 — major is bold on grey, number ends in M (1.3M). List 1 (QC) — major is shaded, number ends in a letter (1.A). Plain on white = minor.
  6. Log every chase: date · driver · issue · resolution. The log is the station’s proof the process was worked.
Defect standard: Schedule 1 · List 1 in Quebec

Filing: smaller stations by driver, monthly · larger operations by TSP, then week
